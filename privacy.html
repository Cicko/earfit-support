Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 2, 2026

EarFit is operated by Rudolf Cicko in the Czech Republic. Contact: rudolfcickodev@gmail.com.

Overview

EarFit is designed to work without an account. Your musical profile, exercise history, accuracy, practice time, XP, streak, settings, and daily workout state stay on your device. EarFit does not transmit this information to us.

Information on your device

EarFit stores the preferences and practice records needed to personalize training, including your onboarding choices, daily goal, reminder preference, exercise answers, response times, adaptive skill statistics, XP, streaks, and local workout state. Removing EarFit removes this local app data, subject to device backups you or Apple manage.

Audio and reminders

Exercises generate audio on your device. EarFit does not record your microphone. Practice reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device; they do not require a server or remote push-notification service.

Feedback

If you choose to send feedback from EarFit, your mail app shows the recipient and message before you send it. We receive only the information you deliberately include in that message and use it to respond to support requests or improve EarFit.

Changes

We may update this policy as EarFit changes. The effective date above will be updated when we do.
